## Platform

## Users

The primary audience is a mix of private customers and organizations planning events in Latvia. Typical uses include weddings, private celebrations, public events, and other occasions that need temporary covered space, furnishings, or supporting event equipment.

Customers may begin with only an event date, location, and approximate need. They use the website to understand the available inventory, compare suitable options, and request a rental arrangement.

## Product Purpose

Liepājas teltis helps customers create a practical, weather-ready event setting by renting tents and complementary inventory. The service combines product selection with delivery and setup, reducing the number of suppliers and logistical steps a customer must coordinate.

Success means helping a customer identify suitable inventory and turn an enquiry into a workable rental arrangement for their event.

## Positioning

Liepājas teltis is a locally based tent and event-inventory rental provider in Liepāja, where the business currently has no directly comparable local competitor. It serves customers throughout Latvia and competes nationally through flexible inventory, competitive pricing, and high phone availability.

The range supports multiple event types rather than specializing only in weddings or a single tent format.

## Operating Context

Customers plan around an event date, venue, guest count, location, and required inventory. They can browse rental categories and products, add relevant items to a cart, submit an enquiry, or contact the business directly.

The service is based in Liepāja and covers Latvia. Delivery and setup are part of the customer proposition. Customers can call at practically any time, including outside conventional office hours, but the website must not imply a guaranteed formal 24/7 service-level agreement unless one is confirmed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Rental categories include tents, flooring, tables and chairs, outdoor heaters, beanbags, and event accessories.
- Tent variety supports weddings, private celebrations, public events, business events, and other uses.
- The service includes delivery and setup.
- Rental enquiries can be submitted online with contact details, event date, location, and requested inventory.
- Product data is loaded from Supabase, with local fallbacks and image overrides in the codebase.
- The public website is in Latvian and serves Latvia.
- Pricing is intended to be competitive; avoid publishing comparative or superlative claims without evidence.
- Availability, final pricing, delivery terms, and setup requirements depend on the individual enquiry unless explicitly stated otherwise.

## Product Principles

1. Make event rental understandable for customers who are not inventory experts.
2. Support many event types and scales without narrowing the product to one occasion.
3. Reduce coordination effort by connecting inventory selection, delivery, and setup.
4. Make direct human contact easy when customers need advice or time-sensitive answers.
5. Keep pricing and service claims specific, credible, and grounded in confirmed terms.
